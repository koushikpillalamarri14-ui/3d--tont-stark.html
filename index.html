From weapons dealer to world's shield

Every genius has an origin measured in loss. Stark's began in a cave, ended in a boardroom, and never really stopped.

The Ten Rings

During a weapons demonstration in Afghanistan, Stark is ambushed and gravely wounded by shrapnel bound for his heart. His captors demand he build them a missile. He builds an electromagnet-powered chest device and armor instead.

A Cave and a Torch

Working from salvaged parts and a car battery, Stark and fellow captive Ho Yinsen assemble the first arc reactor and the crude Mark I armor, escaping under his own power.

Privatizing World Peace

Back home, Stark halts Stark Industries' weapons division overnight, redirects the company toward clean energy and defense technology, and quietly keeps building.
